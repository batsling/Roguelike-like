## How it was made

Every Steam game tagged Roguelike, Roguelite, Action Roguelike, Roguelike Deckbuilder or Traditional Roguelike with **100+ reviews** (the English-review count Steam's search shows): **2,960**, of which **2,193** are not on the chart.

- **Inspiration finds** (part 1): each game's store text and developer announcements were matched with `tools/influence_research.py`'s own claim words and chart-name patterns (native-script titles included). 236 games had a sentence naming a chart game; after reading them all, the ones listed are the developer's own words about this game. Eight more came from interviews and forum posts found by hand, each checked at the source: EVERSPACE, NEO Scavenger, Jump Space, Mullet MadJack, Cuisineer, In Death, GetsuFumaDen: Undying Moon and Killing Room.
- **Deeper passes** over the 1,427 roguelikes and borderline games with no find: the research tool's studio-site and press-kit pass, its itch.io pass and its Bluesky pass, run over these games (every hit read, and each Bluesky poster checked against the game's Steam developer), plus a pass over the Chinese, Japanese and Korean versions of the Steam pages of the 106 games whose title or developer is written in those scripts. Bluesky gave six, studio sites five, the native-language pages one (Night of Full Moon), itch none.
- **Wikipedia:** Wikidata's Steam-app links name the English Wikipedia article of 246 of the off-chart games. Each was scanned the same way and every hit followed to the interview it cites, since Wikipedia is second-hand: six held up (Marvel's Midnight Suns, Tribes of Midgard, TOKOYO, Into the Stars, GALAK-Z, Little Noah), the rest were already listed, comparisons, or a citation that doesn't say it. Wikidata's own "inspired by" statements gave two more (PROJECT TACHYON, Towa), each checked at the reference it carries.
- **Two matching fixes** found along the way, both in `tools/influence_research.py`: bracketed names ("[Dream Quest]") were stripped as markup, and a curly apostrophe ("Don’t Starve") didn't match the sheet's straight one. Re-matching the cached text gave Night of Full Moon and Niche.
- **Same-developer finds** (part 2): each game's developer, from its store page, matched exactly (ignoring Inc., Ltd. and the like) against the developers of chart games. Editions of chart games (prologues, demos, ports, remasters) are left out.
- **Roguelike?** is where a rogue tag sits in the game's own Steam tags, ranked by votes. Calibrated on 79 chart games: 75 of them have one in their top 6, so *yes* = top 6, *borderline* = 7th–11th, *no* = lower or none.

The scripts were one-offs and are not in the repo.
